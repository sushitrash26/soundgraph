# Privacy Policy for SoundGraph

**Last Updated:** October 2026

SoundGraph ("we", "our", or "us") provides a cross-platform music recommendation engine that maps music taste using Graph Data Science and Neo4j.

## Information We Access
When you authenticate with Spotify or Google (YouTube Music), we access:
- **Spotify:** Top tracks, followed artists, saved playlists, and audio feature vectors (tempo, energy, danceability, valence, acousticness).
- **YouTube Music (Google):** Liked music videos, music playlists, and listening history telemetry.

## How We Use Your Information
- To generate your personalized Music Taste Graph.
- To compute Graph Data Science metrics (Node Similarity, Louvain Communities, PageRank).
- To surface explainable cross-platform song and artist recommendations.

## Data Storage & Security
- We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal listening history or telemetry.
- Telemetry is mapped into an encrypted graph instance solely for recommendation calculations.
- You can revoke access at any time through your Google Account Settings or Spotify Connected Apps page.
